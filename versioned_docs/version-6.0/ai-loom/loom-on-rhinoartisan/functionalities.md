# Functionalities

### **1. Rendering Jewelry**

Transform simple photos into clean, polished jewelry images ready to showcase or publish.\
You can render directly from:

* A viewport capture in RhinoArtisan
* A photo of your piece
* A quick sketch or rough image

Supported AI Models: **Google Nano-Banana**, **Reve**, **Fibo**, with different aesthetics and levels of realism.

***

### **2. Rendering From Text**

Generate detailed jewelry images purely from text.\
Describe the shapes, metals, gemstones, enamel, textures, proportions, or mood, and Loom produces an image that follows your description.

Ideal for:

* Concept exploration
* Testing variations without modeling
* Early-stage design communication

***

### **3. Try-On**

Place jewelry onto a person in a photo and obtain a natural, realistic result.\
The AI adjusts:

* Scale
* Perspective
* Lighting
* Skin interaction

Ideal for e-commerce or client previews.

***

### **4. Video**

Create smooth, dynamic short videos from a single image.\
Perfect for:

* Social media
* Online stores
* Quick teasers

Loom adds subtle camera movement and polish to enhance the presentation.

***

### **5. Photo Editor**

Enhance, clean, or adjust your images using a simple prompt.\
No manual tools required.\
You can ask for:

* Brighter metal
* Sharper stones
* Cleaner background
* Better lighting
* Color correction

Loom automatically performs the transformation.

***

### **6. Organic 3D Model from Image**

Generate an **editable 3D model** with organic shapes directly from a photo.\
Designed for:

* Sculptural jewelry
* Organic forms
* Initial 3D base meshes
* Rapid prototyping

The result can be refined in Rhino and finished within your RhinoArtisan workflow.

***

### **7. Sketch From Text**

Turn simple text descriptions into clean, stylized sketches.\
Ideal for:

* Client presentations
* Idea exploration
* Pre-modeling concept work
* Quick mood boards

***

### **8. Organic 3D Model From Text**

Generate an organic 3D model from a textual description.\
Loom produces a sculptural base form that can be:

* Edited
* Refined
* 3D-printed

A perfect tool for early ideation or experimental shapes or use them as a base for further edition.

***

### **AI Models for Each Purpose**

Each Loom functionality can use one or more specialized AI models. You can choose the preferred model depending on:

* Style
* Level of detail
* Type of jewelry
* Aesthetic preferences

For example:

* **Jewelry Rendering** → Google Nano-Banana, Reve, Fibo
* **Text-to-Image** → Google Nano-Banana or Reve
* **Try-On** → Google Nano-Banana, Reve or Fibo
* **3D Generation** → Meshy

This flexibility allows you to create results that match your artistic taste or the technical requirements of your project.
