# Welcome to Loom

Loom is the creative AI system behind RhinoArtisan and RhinoArtisan Connect. Its goal is simple: help you turn your jewelry ideas into stunning visuals in seconds.

With Loom, you can generate images, videos, organic 3D models, sketches, variations, and even try-ons—all without complex tools or technical knowledge. It works directly inside RhinoArtisan or through the standalone Connect app, giving you the flexibility to create anywhere.

In this documentation, you’ll learn how Loom works, what you can create with it, and how to integrate it into your daily workflow.\
Whenever you’re ready, explore the next section to discover everything Loom can do for you.
