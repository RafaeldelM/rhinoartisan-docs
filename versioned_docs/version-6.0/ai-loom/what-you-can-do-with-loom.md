# What you can do with Loom?

Loom opens a new creative world for jewelers, designers, and brands. Instead of limiting you to fixed workflows, it gives you the freedom to explore, experiment, and transform your ideas in seconds. On this page, you’ll find real examples that show the range of what Loom can do—from turning simple screenshots into luxury renders to generating videos, try-ons, new angles, variations, and fully imagined compositions. And now, let us show you some of the things Loom can create for you.

### From Screenshot to Luxury Render

From a simple capture inside RhinoArtisan, Loom transforms your design into a studio-grade visual.

We start by creating a new Loom project and generating an ultra-realistic render: refined rose gold, high-clarity diamonds, perfect light dispersion, and precise prong detailing. Loom even creates the matching pair automatically, ensuring both earrings share the exact same style, materials, reflections, and proportions. A clean white reflective background enhances brilliance and delivers a premium, minimal studio look.

### Consistent Gold Variations in Seconds

Need variations? Just ask. Using the original image, Loom generates two additional versions—instantly.\
Version one: only the metal changes to white gold.\
Version two: only the metal shifts to yellow gold.

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/rhinoartisan_vi (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/rhinoartisan_vi (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Everything else remains identical: lighting, camera angle, reflections, diamonds, and background.\
Perfectly consistent results, ready for catalogs, lookbooks, or A/B testing.

### Realistic Try-On, Instantly

Bring your jewelry to life on a real person. Loom can take the earring and place it naturally on the ear of a blonde woman, respecting skin tones, shadows, proportions, and perspective.\
It creates a clean, realistic try-on image, ideal for presentations and online stores.
