# Who Is Loom For?

Loom is designed for anyone in the jewelry world who needs to create high-quality visuals quickly, easily, and with total creative freedom. Whether you design, sell, manufacture, or present jewelry, Loom adapts to your workflow and amplifies your results.

#### Jewelry Designers

Turn early ideas into polished visuals in seconds. From sketches to photorealistic renders, Loom helps you explore forms, materials, and concepts without slowing down the creative process.

#### Retailers & Sales Teams

Create stunning images, variations, and try-ons to help customers visualize their piece instantly. Perfect for in-store experiences, online catalogs, and personalized presentations.

#### Marketing & Social Media Teams

Produce campaign-ready images, videos, and vertical formats directly from a single photo. Keep your content fresh, consistent, and perfectly adapted to every platform.

#### Manufacturers & CAD Professionals

Generate renderings, angle variations, or organic 3D bases to support approvals, technical reviews, or prototyping. Loom becomes a fast visual companion for production workflows.

#### Brands & Creative Directors

Explore new styles, materials, and art directions in seconds. Loom lets you test ideas, build moodboards, and produce concepts without needing full photo shoots or rendering sessions.

#### Students & Educators

Provide access to professional-level visuals without the need for complex tools or expensive hardware. Loom helps students bring their projects to life instantly.

#### Anyone Creating Jewelry Content

If you need beautiful visuals—renders, videos, try-ons, concepts—Loom gives you the fastest and most flexible way to create them.
