# What is Loom

Loom is the artificial intelligence system that powers RhinoArtisan and RhinoArtisan Connect. It has been designed to take advantage of the most powerful AI models available today and turn them into a creative platform capable of generating images, videos, sketches, and organic 3D models from your jewelry designs.

Loom is completely free to use. You can access all tools and features at no cost. When you generate images, videos, or 3D models, Loom automatically connects to specialized services that host and run these AI models—such as fal.ai, Nano Banana, Veo3, Klining, and many others. These services operate on a pay-per-use basis, and you pay them directly, with no commissions or added fees from RhinoArtisan.

This architecture provides total flexibility: Loom offers the infrastructure, integration, and creative experience, while you decide which models to use and how much you want to invest in each generation.

More than a tool, Loom is a bridge between the jewelry world and the most advanced AI technologies, delivering a powerful, accessible, and versatile way to create professional-quality content in seconds.
