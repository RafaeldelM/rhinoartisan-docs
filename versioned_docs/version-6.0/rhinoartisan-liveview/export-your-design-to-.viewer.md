# Export your design to .viewer

### Step 1: **Export your design to .viewer format.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/FILE_SAVE_VIEWER (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> From RhinoArtisan, click the **Viewer** button, located in the File menu, to export your design as a `.viewer` file.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (758).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

RhinoArtisan automatically estimates the file size based on the selected resolution quality—from Highest to Fast—allowing the user to choose the option that best balances detail and performance.

### **Step 2: Upload your `.viewer` file**

Host the `.viewer` file on your web server, CDN, or storage system (e.g., AWS S3, your e-commerce backend, or internal ERP system).
