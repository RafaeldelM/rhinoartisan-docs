# How to Embed

### **How to Embed the RhinoArtisan LiveView**

_The 3D jewelry experience that fits anywhere._

RhinoArtisan LiveView is a next-generation viewer built as a **Web Component**, making it incredibly easy to integrate into any website, platform, or app—no matter the technology stack.

**Why Web Component?**\
Because you shouldn’t have to rebuild your site just to offer a premium 3D experience.\
Web Components are the future of modular web development. They allow RhinoArtisan LiveView to be:

* ✅ **Framework-agnostic** – Works seamlessly with Angular, React, Vue, plain HTML, or any CMS like WordPress or Shopify.
* ⚡ **Lightweight & fast** – Loads only what it needs. No bulky dependencies.
* 🎨 **Fully customizable** – Style it to match your brand effortlessly.
* 🔐 **Secure & isolated** – Runs in its own scope, avoiding conflicts with your site.
* 📱 **Mobile-ready** – Optimized for all devices, from desktop to smartphone.

Whether you're building an e-commerce site, a B2B platform, or a digital showroom, **LiveView** delivers a beautiful, immersive way to showcase your jewelry—**without compromising performance or design flexibility.**

**Integrate once. Impress always.**

The **Viewer** is a **3D viewer in HTML format** that can be **embedded into any website, e-commerce platform, ERP, application, or software supporting web content with just a single line of code**.

This viewer allows users to **interact with the 3D design**: move it, zoom in/out, rotate, and explore it from any angle with great detail.

The **API** provides a layer of customization and control over how the viewer behaves when integrated into different environments. Through the API, it is possible to:

* Configure **materials**: define colors for metals and gems.
* Enable interactive tools such as:
  * **Zoom**
  * **Snapshot** (image capture)
  * **Background color change**
* **Control animations**: start, speed up, slow down, or stop the design’s movement.

***

**Step 3: Use this HTML snippet**

Replace `YOUR_FILE_URL.viewer` with the actual URL of your `.viewer` file:

```html
htmlCopyEdit<script src="https://viewer.rhinoartisan.com/embed.js" data-viewer="https://yourdomain.com/files/YOUR_FILE_URL.viewer"></script>
```

&#x20;**That’s it!** With this single line, the interactive 3D viewer will appear in your web page or app.
