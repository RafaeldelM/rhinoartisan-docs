## What is RhinoArtisan LiveView?

RhinoArtisan LiveView is a next-generation viewer built as a **Web Component**, making it incredibly easy to integrate into any website, platform, or app—no matter the technology stack.

RhinoArtisan Viewer HTML is built for jewelry brands that want to deliver a premium, interactive experience—online or in-store. Showcase your 3D designs directly on your website or digital catalog, with no dependencies, no installations, and full cross-device compatibility.

**Fast to load. Easy to integrate. Fully brandable.**

Perfect for:

* High-end jewelry e-commerce
* B2B platforms
* Digital catalogs
* Interactive showrooms
* In-store touch displays

### **Why Web Component?**

Because you shouldn’t have to rebuild your site just to offer a premium 3D experience.\
Web Components are the future of modular web development. They allow RhinoArtisan LiveView to be:

* ✅ **Framework-agnostic** – Works seamlessly with Angular, React, Vue, plain HTML, or any CMS like WordPress or Shopify.
* ⚡ **Lightweight & fast** – Loads only what it needs. No bulky dependencies.
* 🎨 **Fully customizable** – Style it to match your brand effortlessly.
* 🔐 **Secure & isolated** – Runs in its own scope, avoiding conflicts with your site.
* 📱 **Mobile-ready** – Optimized for all devices, from desktop to smartphone.

Whether you're building an e-commerce site, a B2B platform, or a digital showroom, **LiveView** delivers a beautiful, immersive way to showcase your jewelry—**without compromising performance or design flexibility.**

**Integrate once. Impress always.**
