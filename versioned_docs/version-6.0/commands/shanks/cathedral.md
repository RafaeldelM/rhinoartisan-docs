# Cathedral

## <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/RING_ETERNITY.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Cathedral

Using this command, you can create cathedral shanks, especially useful to design fancy and vintage engagement rings with arms raising to support the gemsets on top.

![](/img/v60/image 208.png>)

Running this command will display its parameters in the Commands toolbar. Your first step should be to choose whether you want to start working on one of the styles offered by RhinoArtisan in the gallery of this command or to create a new one by editing the one that it is shown in the viewport.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Cathedral (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On its parameters, you can find the ring size, measurements and the asset that will give shape to the shank, and options to add secondary gems with their setting style on this element among other possibilities.

When you confirm your changes, the Cathedral Shank will be listed on the Outliner toolbar.

:::info
Goldsmith Tip : This type of shank is designed to support a basket, halo, bezel, or peg-head, which will hold the ring's center stone. It's important not to finalize the parameters of the cathedral shank until the central stone's setting is complete. Any changes to the stone's position or its setting may require adjustments to the shank's design to ensure a cohesive and aesthetically pleasing result that harmonizes both elements.
:::

### **Prongs**

### <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/PRONGS_NONE (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Without prongs**&#x20;

This option excludes prongs from being added to the gems, providing a clean design without any additional settings for prong customization.

### <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/PRONGS_SHARED (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Add Prongs**

This option includes prongs for the gems and provides several parameters for customization:

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (660).png" alt="" width="283"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* **Gem Inside**: Controls the horizontal level at which the prong interacts with the gem.
* **Height**: Adjusts the vertical height of the prong.
* **Extension Manufacturing (only in manufacturing mode):** Manages the extension of the prong from the topside, useful in the manufacturing stage.
* **Move Z:** Modifies the height position of the prong along the Z-axis.
* **Automatic Prong Diameter**: Toggles the automatic adjustment of the prong’s diameter based on the gem's dimensions.
* **Diameter**: Controls the diameter of the prong.

### <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/PRONGS_SCALLOPED (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Cutters**

The **Cutters** section allows users to establish cutting options and parameters for both standard and V-Cutters.

**V-Cutters**: Toggles between enabling V-Cutters.

**Boolean**: Toggles between enabling the cutters boolean.

**Remove Channels**: Toggles between removing cutters channel.

#### **Cutters Parameters**

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (661).png" alt="" width="290"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* **Start | Height**: Controls the top height of the cutters.
* **Start | Width**: Controls the top width of the cutters.
* **End | Height**: Controls the bottom height of the cutters.
* **End | Width**: Controls the bottom width of the cutters.
* **Length**: Controls the length of the cutters.
* **Move in Z**: Controls the vertical placement.
* **Move Middle in Z**: Controls the center height of the Cutters.
* **Shapes**: Toggles between the shapes in the cutters.
* **Mode**: Toggles between the independence of the cutters towards the Start/End parameters.
* **Shape Scale**: Controls the spectrum between round and squared shape by a slider.

**V-Cutters**

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (662).png" alt="" width="290"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* **Start | Width**: Controls the top width of the V-Cutters.
* **End | Height**: Controls the bottom height of the V-Cutters.
* **End | Width**: Controls the bottom width of the V-Cutters.
* **Length**: Controls the length of the V-Cutters.
* **Move in Z**: Controls the vertical placement.
* **Mode**: Toggles between the independence of the V-Cutters towards the Start/End parameters.
