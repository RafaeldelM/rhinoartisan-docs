# Line

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/G1_02_DRAW_LINE.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Line

The Line command draws one line segment.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_01_DRAW_POLYLINE (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Polyline

The Polyline command draws a series of joined line or arc segments.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_02_DRAW_FROM_MIDLANE (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> From Midpoint

Draw one line segment from its midpoint.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_03_DRAW_NORMAL_SURFACE (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Normal To Surface

Draw a line segment normal to a surface.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_04_DRAW_BISECTOR (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Bisector

Draw one line segment at a bisecting angle.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_05_DRAW_VERTICAL_CPLANE (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Vertical To CPlane

Draw a line segment perpendicular to the construction plane.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_06_DRAW_ANGLED (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Angled

Draw one angled line segment.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_07_DRAW_PERPEDICULAR (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Perpendicular From Curve

Draw a line segment perpendicular from a curve.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_08_DRAW_PERPEDICULAR_2_LINES (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Perpendicular To 2 Curves

Draw one line segment perpendicular to two curves.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_09_DRAW_TANGENT (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Tangent From Curve

Draw a line segment tangent from a curve.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_10_DRAW_TANGENT_2_CURVES (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Tangent To 2 Curves

Draw one line segment tangent to two curves.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/LINE_11_DRAW_TANGENT_PERPEDICULAR (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Tangent, Perpendicular

Draw one line segment tangent and perpendicular to curves.
