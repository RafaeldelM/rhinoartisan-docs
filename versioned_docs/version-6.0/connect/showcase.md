# Showcase

The **Showcase** within the **Connect** platform provides a centralized and intuitive space to **explore the models in your published collections**. It serves as a dynamic showcase where your organization’s collections come to life, enabling you to share design catalogs seamlessly with your team while ensuring immediate access to the latest approved creations.

This section transforms portfolio management by enabling:

* **Real-time visualization of published collections**, displaying your models without the need for file exports or external tools.
* **Exclusive access for members of your organization**, offering a secure and private environment where collaboration and client presentations flow effortlessly.
* A structured and elegant presentation that enhances your showcases, helping you impress clients with up-to-date and professionally curated catalogs.
