Yes, we accept requests for new features or custom integrations, especially for companies with specific needs. If you have an idea, a special requirement, or need to integrate RhinoArtisan Connect with a particular tool, just contact us and we’ll explore the best way to tailor the platform to your needs.\
Our development team reviews all proposals and, depending on feasibility, we can offer custom solutions—especially in the Enterprise plans.
