# Embed on your web

In the same Share to Web panel, you’ll also find an **Embed Code**:

\<iframe src="https://connect.rhinoartisan.com/link/YourModel" width="100%" height="100%" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Group 7597 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Copy and paste this iframe snippet into your website to embed the interactive viewer directly onto any page. This allows clients or visitors to rotate, zoom, and experience the model right within your branded environment.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/web (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This set of export tools makes it simple to present, promote, and share your designs professionally—whether it’s a still image, an interactive viewer, or a full video showcase.
