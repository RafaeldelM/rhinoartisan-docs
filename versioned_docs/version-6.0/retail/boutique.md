# Boutique

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/FILE_BOUTIQUE_3D.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Boutique&#x20;

### What is the Boutique module in RhinoArtisan?

**Boutique** is a library of ready-made jewelry collections, designed to provide inspiration and high-quality starting points for your own creations. It includes a wide variety of professional models that can be customized, edited, or sent directly to production.



In addition to exploring the collections provided by RhinoArtisan, you can also create your own collections and organize your designs just the way you want. These custom collections can be shared with your clients, making it easy to present your work and streamline the decision-making process.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (750).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### How it works ?

The first time you click the Boutique button, the system will ask if you’d like to **download the available collections**. These include content for both the **Boutique** and **Components** modules.

Once downloaded:

* Collections are automatically installed in your user folder.
* They become accessible within the Boutique window.
* You’ll see them organized with **cover images**, **titles**, and **short descriptions**.

When you open a collection, the window will display all included designs. Each model appears with its image, name, and description. Clicking a design will insert it directly into the RhinoArtisan viewport, ready for customization or production.

The Boutique window stays open, so you can continue browsing and inserting models without interruption.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (744).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Selecting a design

* Click on any design to load it instantly into your RhinoArtisan scene.
* Once inserted, the model can be:
  * Edited or customized.
  * Exported as an STL file for 3D printing or manufacturing.
  * Sent directly to a client or production team.
* You can keep exploring the Boutique while working, thanks to the persistent window.
