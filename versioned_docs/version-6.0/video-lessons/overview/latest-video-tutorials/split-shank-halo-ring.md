# Split Shank Halo Ring

Welcome to RhinoArtisan! In this tutorial, we’ll guide you through a complete, production-ready workflow for creating a **split shank ring with a cushion-cut halo center stone**. Starting from a precise ring curve, you’ll learn how to position the center gem, build a clean halo with cutters and rails, and sculpt a split shank that flows naturally into the setting.

This tutorial focuses on practical jewelry CAD techniques: curve-driven construction, controlled solid operations, and optimized stone settings. You’ll finish with a lightweight, manufacturing-ready ring that balances elegance, structural integrity, and efficient metal usage.
