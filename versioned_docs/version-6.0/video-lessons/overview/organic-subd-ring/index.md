# Organic SubD Ring

Master the art of creating fluid, organic ring designs with the Organic SubD Ring Design course. This collection guides you through crafting elegant, nature-inspired basket settings that balance beauty and structural strength. Learn advanced SubD modeling techniques in RhinoArtisan, including intricate cushion and round baskets, seamless connections, and perfect symmetry using mirroring and refinement tools. Transition smoothly from SubD to NURBS for precision and polish. The course also focuses on real-world functionality, covering professional rendering preparation, detailed cutters, Boolean operations, and material adjustments to enhance gemstone brilliance. Elevate your jewelry design with cutting-edge techniques and nature’s elegance.
