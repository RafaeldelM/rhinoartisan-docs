# Hoop Earrings

Master the process of designing elegant, professional-grade hoop earrings with this comprehensive tutorial. Starting from basic curves, you’ll learn how to bring a sophisticated pair of earrings to life using RhinoArtisan’s powerful features. This lesson focuses on practical and aesthetic precision—perfect for designers aiming to expand their skills in gemstone placement and structural detailing.

Discover how to harness tools like _Gem by Curve_ to achieve exact alignment and spacing, streamline your workflow with automated prong and cutter generation, and present your final design with eye-catching renders. Whether you're developing a new collection or refining your technical abilities, this tutorial offers the perfect balance of creativity and control.
