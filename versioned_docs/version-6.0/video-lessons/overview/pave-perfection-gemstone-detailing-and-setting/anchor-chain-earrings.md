# Anchor Chain Earrings

This tutorial explores the fusion of bold structural design and fine pavé detailing—perfect for CAD jewelry designers aiming to create standout earrings with a luxury finish.\
Dive into the design of full pavé anchor chain earrings using RhinoArtisan’s powerful tools to streamline complexity and enhance elegance. You’ll begin with a custom-built anchor link and learn how to multiply and adapt the form with precision. The course also covers the creation of a detailed gallery for added refinement and the integration of a secure Omega clasp for professional-grade functionality.\
Master the Automatic Pavé command to place diamonds efficiently and symmetrically across curved surfaces, achieving a polished and cohesive look throughout your piece.
