# Sapphire Eternity Ring

Welcome to RhinoArtisan! In this tutorial, we’ll guide you step by step through the complete process of creating a stunning diamond eternity ring in RhinoArtisan. From building precise curves and references, to designing the shank, carving the channel, and adding perfectly arranged pavé-set diamonds, you’ll master a professional, production-ready jewelry workflow. We’ll cover advanced curve construction, channel creation, gem placement with symmetry and arrays, prongs, and cutters — ending with a clean, polished model ready for manufacturing or rendering.
