# Solitaire Ring

This tutorial will teach you how to design a engagement ring. You'll create a classic tapered shank, adjusting the profile, width, and height to your specifications. Then, you'll design a 1-carat diamond and place it in a custom basket, modifying its height, diameter, and rail profiles for a perfect fit. Finally, you'll apply materials like 18k white gold for the band and diamond material for the gem, and set up a scene to render the final design.
