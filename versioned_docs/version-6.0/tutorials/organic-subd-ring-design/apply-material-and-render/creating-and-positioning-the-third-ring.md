# Creating and Positioning the Third Ring

### **Create the Third Ring**

Select the first ring, then press **Alt** while using the **Gumball** to drag the copy to the right along the X-axis.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/LastCopie.png)

Rotate the third ring by **-45 degrees** as a reference.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Rotate45.png)

### **Position the Third Ring**

Position the third ring to rest on the upper ring.

Rotate the ring as needed, ensuring it rests at the same height as the bottom ring and does not overlap with the others. This ensures realistic shadowing in the final render.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/POSE-LAST.png)
