# The Library

The **Library** is a key feature in KeyShot Studio, offering a wide array of pre-defined resources to expedite the rendering process.

**Materials**

Access an extensive collection of materials for metals, gemstones, and textures, simulating real-world properties for photorealistic results.

Additional material packs (e.g., precious metals and gemstones) are available on the RhinoArtisan website.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Textures**

Use texture maps to add details like patterns, reliefs, and imperfections for enhanced realism.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Environments**

Choose HDRI environments to simulate various lighting conditions, from studio setups to outdoor scenes, impacting reflections and shadows.

* Specific jewelry-focused environments can also be downloaded from RhinoArtisan.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Models**

Incorporate 3D models, such as display stands, to enrich scenes and create realistic setups.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Colors**

Access standard color palettes for consistent background tones and material adjustments.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Library is customizable, allowing designers to add specific resources to create stunning visuals of their jewelry designs.
