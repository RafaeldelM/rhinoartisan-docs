# From RhinoArtisan to Keyshot



### What is Jewelry Rendering and Its Purpose

Discover the art and purpose of jewelry rendering. Learn how this essential process helps visualize designs in photorealistic quality, aiding in presentation, marketing, and client approvals.

### Introduction to KeyShot Studio

Get familiar with KeyShot Studio, a powerful tool for creating high-quality renders. Understand its key features and why it’s the go-to software for jewelry design.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (772).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Exploring the KeyShot Interface

Navigate through KeyShot’s intuitive interface. Learn about its key areas, from the material library to the render settings, making the rendering process seamless.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (773).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Hands-On Practical Exercise

* **Importing Designs**: Bring your first RhinoArtisan design into KeyShot Studio.
* **Applying Materials**: Experiment with textures and finishes to give your design life.
* **Lighting Basics**: Adjust the lighting to highlight details and enhance realism.
* **Environment Settings**: Create a backdrop that complements your design and elevates its presentation.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (774).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Creating a Professional Jewelry Render

Learn how to configure and fine-tune your settings to produce a stunning professional-grade render, capturing the brilliance of your design in high resolution.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image 1239.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### What You’ll Need

RhinoArtisan: To design your jewelry models.

KeyShot Studio: For rendering and finalizing your designs. No prior experience is needed—we’ll guide you step by step to create breathtaking jewelry visuals.
