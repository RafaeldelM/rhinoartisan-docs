# Rendering the Earrings

**Objective:** Generate a high-quality image for sharing or client presentation.

**Render Settings**

In KeyShot, click the **Render button**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (58).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Set the following:

* **Output Name**: Choose a descriptive name for the image (2).
* **Location:** Select a folder to save your image(3).
* **Resolution**: Select your desired resolution (4).

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (59).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Go to **Options** (1).
* **Number of Samples**: Set to 200 for optimal quality.
* Click **Render** and monitor progress.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (60).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Once complete, the render will be saved in your location folder.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (61).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Congratulations!**

You have successfully rendered a pair of earrings with realistic materials and lighting in KeyShot Studio. Now you can experiment with different materials and designs to create even more stunning visuals.
