# Apply materials and Render



### **Step 1: Access the Render and Animation Panel**

To begin, go to the **Render and Animation** panel located on the right side of the interface.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-1219-1024x307.png)

Alternatively, you can access this panel by going to the **Rendering** tab and clicking on the **Render and Animation** command, located on the far right.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-51-1024x120.jpg)

### **Step 2: Apply Rose Gold**

In the Render and Animation tab, apply a Rose Gold 18k material to the shank.

<figure><img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-18-10h28m05s441.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **Step 2: Apply White Gold**

White Gold 18k material to the bezel.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-18-10h28m25s635.png)

### **Step 3:** Ruby material to the gem

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-18-10h28m34s013.png)

### **Step 4: Ground Material**

Basic Reflection Brown material to the ground.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-18-10h28m41s581.png)

### **Step 5: Render**

Select the parameters for the render:

* 3840×2160 (16:9)
* 200 Quality

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-18-10h28m57s460.png)
