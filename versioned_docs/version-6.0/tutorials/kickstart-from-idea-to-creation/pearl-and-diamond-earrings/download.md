### Download

To start, open the provided 3DM file titled 'Pearl and Diamond Earrings' for this tutorial.



You will see two elements: a gem with a basket setting and a subD.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-16-12h11m19s752.jpg)

### Curator

On the left side, you will notice a panel with different elements categorized by types. This panel is called Curator and allows us to have a library of elements provided by RhinoArtisan as well as your own library.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Stud3-1024x396.png)

You can also find the Curator panel **(2)** in the Components tab **(1)**.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Group-7211.jpg)
