# Apply Materials and Render

### **Render and Animation Panel**

To begin, go to the **Render and Animation** panel located on the right side of the interface.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-1219-1024x307.png)

Alternatively, you can access this panel by going to the **Rendering** tab and clicking on the **Render and Animation** command, located on the far right.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-51-1024x120.jpg)

### **Step 1: Pearl Material**

Apply the pearl material to the pearl.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-15h47m20s272.png)

### **Step 2: Diamond Material**

The diamond material to the gems.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-15h47m34s408.png)

### **Step 3: Hide**

To simplify the process, select the gems and pearls and type "Hide" in the command bar.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-15h48m04s317.png)

### **Step 4: White Gold Material**

Now select the metallic elements and apply the White Gold 14k material.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-15h49m25s940.png)

### **Step 5: Show**

Show the hidden elements by typing "Show" in the command bar.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-15h44m07s090.png)

### **Step 6:  Parameters**

Select the parameters for the render:

* 3840x2160 (16:9)
* 200 Quality

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-15h41m45s558.png)

### **Step 7:  Render**

Press the Render Button

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-16-11h40m14s524-e1721124035369.png)
