# Choosing the Basket Setting

In this lesson, we will design a curved basket setting for your diamond. RhinoArtisan offers a variety of pre-designed basket models that you can customize to fit your needs. Let's explore the steps involved in selecting and customizing your basket setting.

<figure><img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-1181-1024x556.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 1: Basket Command

Begin by select the gem and then navigate to the toolbar and select the “Diamonds” tab **(1)**. Select the “Basket” command **(2)**. This will open a library of pre-designed basket models.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/BasketSetting-1-1024x77.png)

### Step 2: Basket Library

From the library, choose the BA00&#x32;**(1)** basket model. This model is ideal for this tutorial as it allows us to create a curved design, which is both functional and aesthetically pleasing.

Click on the pencil icon **(2)** at the top of the library to open the editing panel. This will allow you to adjust the various parameters of the basket.



![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Group-7200-1.jpg)

Since the half-carat stone has a diameter larger than 5 millimeters, it’s crucial to ensure that the prongs securely hold the diamond.

### **Step 3: Parameters**

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Parameters-1.jpg)

* Set the prong **diameter** to **1.20**.
* To enhance the design and add detail, set the **bottom diameter** of the lower part of the basket to **0.90**. This will ensure that the basket is both sturdy and visually appealing.
* Long earrings can affect balance when worn, so it’s important to get the height right. Set the **height** of the basket to **4.3**. This height is optimal for setting the stone securely while leaving enough space for the setting.
* Change the prong mode to **curved** for a more elegant design.
* Set the post support to **Double** to provide additional stability for the setting.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Frame-1-1024x430.png)
