# Apply Materials and Rendering

With all components in place, our Classic Cluster Ring is ready for rendering. At this point, we’ll visualize the design and check that all elements fit together perfectly.

### **Render panel**

To create the final render, go to the Render and Animation panel.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/RenderAndAnimation-Panel-1024x106.png)

### **Metals**

* Select the Cluster and type “Ungroup” (1) in the command bar.&#x20;
* Use the layer panel to select all the metals. Right click on the metal color (2).
* Apply Yellow gold 18k (3).

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/LayersSelect-Metal_2-2.png)

### **Gems**

* Use the Layers Panel to select the gems and apply a diamond material to them.
* Choose your preferred ground style under the Ground category.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/LayersSelect-GEMS-1.png)

### Perspective and Resolution

Adjust the perspective angle and distance to frame your render. Set your preferred resolution and quality, then click the Render button.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/REnder_halo-1.png)

Your Cluster Ring design is ready to showcase! Share it on social media for all to see.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/render2.png)
