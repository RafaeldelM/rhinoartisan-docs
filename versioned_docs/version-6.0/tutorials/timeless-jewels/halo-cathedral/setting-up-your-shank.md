# Setting Up Your Shank

### **Step 1: Cathedral Command**

Go to the **Shanks** tab (1), select the **Cathedral** (2) command, and click on **Edit**.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Cathedral-Command-1024x141.png)

### **Step 2: Ring Size**

Choose the **United States region** and select **Finger Size 6 1/2**.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Cathedral-Size-1024x370.png)

### Step 3: Shaded Mode

To improve model visibility, go to the bottom-right corner of the viewport and choose **Shaded Display Mode**.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Shaded-1024x370.png)

### **Step 4: Profile Selector**

Click on the **Profile preview**. In the **Asset Selector** (1), select profile **006**.

Click on edit the profile, and edit the profile to match the image.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Profile-EDitor-1024x318.png)

### **Step 5: Adjust Parameters**

* Top width: 2.3 mm
* Top height: 1.9 mm
* Bottom width: 3.0 mm
* Bottom height: 1.9 mm

### **Step 6: Set Cathedral Shank**

* Width: 6.0 mm
* Height 1.7 mm
* Angle: 80º
* Tangent to 0.

### **Step 7: Set Bridge Dimensions**

* Configure **Width** to 1.6 mm
* **Thickness** to 0.8 mm
* **Diameter** to 0 mm.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Parameters-1024x282.png)

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Shank-Cathedral-finish-1024x344.png)

Click **Accept** in the top-right to save your modifications.
