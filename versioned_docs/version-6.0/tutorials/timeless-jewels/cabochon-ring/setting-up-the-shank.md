# Setting Up the Shank

### **Begin in the Shanks Tab**

In the **Shanks** tab (1), select the **Signet** (2) command in the **Shanks** tab.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Signet.png)

### **Switch to Shaded Mode**

For better visualization, switch to **Shaded Mode** by clicking on the bottom-right corner of each viewport.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Signet_shaded.png)

### **Edit Profile**

* Without selecting any element, click on **Edit**.
* Click the **profile preview** (1) and select **Profile 003** (2).

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Signet_profile.png)

### **Set Dimensions**

* **Width**: 10 mm
* **Height**: 12 mm
* **Height Position**: 2.86 mm
* **Bottom Width**: 2.35 mm
* **Bottom Height**: 1.5 mm
* **Middle Width**: 2.7 mm
* **Middle Height**: 1.8 mm

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Signet_parameters.png)

### **Recess Option**

Note that the **Recess** feature is ideal for emblems or initials but for cabochon stones, we’ll use the **Boolean method** to achieve a snug fit.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Signet_recess.png)
