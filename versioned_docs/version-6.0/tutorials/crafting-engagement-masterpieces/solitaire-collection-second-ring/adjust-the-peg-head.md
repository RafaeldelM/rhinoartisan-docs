# Adjust the Peg Head

### **Step 1: Edit the Setting**

Select the peg head and press **Edit**.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Peghead-Adjust-1024x293.png)

Switch to the **Front View** to check how the peg head fits with the shank.

### **Step 3: Parameters**

Adjust the **height** of the peg head until it is hidden and no longer protrudes below the shank (4.7 mm).&#x20;

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Peghead-Adjust2-1024x495.png)
