# Render and Pose

### **Tips: Gems Render**

_For better visualization of the gems in the render, use a cutter to remove the metal part that intersects with the gem. This way, the light can interact properly, and the render will look more realistic._&#x20;



### **Step 1: Apply Materials**

In the **Render and Animation** tab, assign materials to your model:

* For the metals, apply **White Gold 18k**.
* For the gem, use the **Diamond** material.
* Select a ground material like **Basic Reflection Blue** to enhance the render.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-19-1024x180.png)

### **Step 2: Render**

Adjust the rendering parameters to achieve the best results. Complete the rendering process.
