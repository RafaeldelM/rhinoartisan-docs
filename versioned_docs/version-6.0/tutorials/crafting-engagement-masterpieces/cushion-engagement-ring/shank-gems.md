# Shank Gems

### **Steps 1: Shank Gems**&#x20;

Go to the "Gems" tab within the shank settings. Set the drill height to 2 millimeters.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/L5-Shank-4-1024x506.png)

### **Step 2: Stability**

Ensure the shank tips intersect perfectly with the upper rail of the basket for stability.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/L5-Shank-5-1024x506.png)

**Next Step:** Apply materials to the ring.
