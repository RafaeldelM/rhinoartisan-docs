# Nivoda

This panel allows you to set your **preferences** regarding certified stones — such as stone type, origin, certification, and supplier filters. Based on these preferences, RhinoArtisan will automatically suggest up to **three matching options** from Nivoda’s stock in the **Breakdown Details** section, helping you choose the best available stones for your design in terms of quality and price.
