# 1. Add your license on Cloud Zoo

We will proceed to add the RhinoArtisan license in Cloud Zoo. <br>

1. In our case, we will access our RhinoArtisan team, although it can be any team.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2024-10-24 11_03_23-Rhino – Choose a Team_ Perfil 1 - Microsoft​ Edge.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

As shown in the following image, we already have the Rhinoceros license. To add the RhinoArtisan license, please click on the **Add License** button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2024-10-24 10_44_11-Rhino – Licenses_ Perfil 1 - Microsoft​ Edge.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



:::info
**Very important:** If your RhinoArtisan product key does not start with **RA60**, there is no problem; you can add it at the beginning.
:::

In the **License Key field**, enter your RhinoArtisan license. Click on the **Add License** button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2024-10-24 10_46_34-Rhino – Add Licenses to Cloud Zoo_ Perfil 1 - Microsoft​ Edge.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Your RhinoArtisan license is now available in Cloud Zoo.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2024-10-24 10_46_47-Rhino – Licenses_ Perfil 1 - Microsoft​ Edge.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
