# Welcome to Assistant

Assistant is your intelligent companion inside RhinoArtisan, created to help you work faster, with more clarity and less friction throughout the jewelry design process. It is not a design tool itself, but a powerful layer that supports, guides and automates many of the tasks that surround creation.

Whether you work with customers in-store, build collections in the studio, or manage projects and decisions, Assistant helps you interact with your jewelry ecosystem in a smarter way. It understands your libraries, assists you through workflows, retrieves technical information, checks production details, prepares reports, supports rendering and 3D printing preparation, and helps you reuse what already exists.



Assistant does not create jewelry models for you — it is still learning — but it becomes an extension of your work by organizing designs, suggesting relevant content from Boutique, Curator and Components, and helping you move quickly from idea to decision without unnecessary steps.

:::tip
100% privacy. 100% independence.
:::

Assistant runs entirely on your computer. It is included with RhinoArtisan and requires no internet connection. Built with open-source technologies, it works fully offline and does not send any information to 2Shapes or to any external service. Your data, designs and activity remain private on your machine at all times.

:::info
Please check that your computer meets the technical requirements to run Assistant.
:::

Assistant is designed to amplify your expertise, reduce manual effort, and let you focus on creativity and decision-making.
