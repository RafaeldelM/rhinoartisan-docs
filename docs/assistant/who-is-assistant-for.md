# Who Is Assistant For?

Assistant is built to support the main roles involved in jewelry design and sales. Each role benefits differently, but they all share the same goal: working faster, making better decisions, and getting more value from existing jewelry designs.

Assistant does not generate jewelry from scratch. Instead, it helps you search, reuse, adapt and extract information from the thousands of designs already available in your RhinoArtisan ecosystem, including Boutique, Curator and Components.

### 1. Retailers - Design Jewelry With Your Customers

Assistant enhances the in-store experience by helping sales teams quickly find, adapt and present jewelry based on existing designs. It allows retailers to co-create visually with customers without needing technical knowledge.

What retailers can do with Assistant:

* Search and reuse designs from Boutique instantly
* Adapt styles and variations based on existing models
* Show visual options during the sales conversation
* Provide fast answers about details and possibilities
* Create a collaborative and engaging buying experience

Assistant helps transform the conversation, but the jewelry always comes from your real design libraries - not from automatic creation.

### 2. Designers - Especially Junior Designers Who Need Speed and Structure

Assistant is a productivity accelerator for designers, with a strong focus on helping junior designers work more efficiently and confidently. Instead of starting from zero, designers can build on proven models that already exist.

Assistant helps designers:

* Locate the right base designs across large libraries
* Reuse Components and Curator assets intelligently
* Generate variations from existing structures
* Avoid repetitive setup work
* Stay aligned with collection standards
* Learn workflows faster through guided assistance

This is not magic design generation. Assistant works with what already exists and helps transform it, adapt it and organize it so designers can move much faster and focus on refinement rather than rebuilding.

### 3. C-Level & Managers - Access Design Information Without Rhino

Assistant gives executives and decision-makers direct access to meaningful design information without needing CAD expertise or relying on designers to extract data. It turns complex RhinoArtisan projects into clear, business-ready insights.

What managers can do:

* Instantly read weights and material breakdowns
* Access gemstone details and measurements
* Generate renders and visuals from existing designs
* Produce reports and technical summaries automatically
* Review collections without navigating Rhino tools
* Compare design options based on real production data
* Make faster decisions with total clarity

Assistant works on top of jewelry already created in RhinoArtisan, translating technical design data into clear and actionable information. It does not create models - it helps leadership understand and evaluate them quickly and independently.

## A Unified Experience

Assistant connects your entire jewelry workflow by acting as an intelligent layer over your design libraries. Whether you are selling, designing, or managing, it helps you work smarter by leveraging what your team has already built.

It doesn’t replace creativity. It amplifies it.
