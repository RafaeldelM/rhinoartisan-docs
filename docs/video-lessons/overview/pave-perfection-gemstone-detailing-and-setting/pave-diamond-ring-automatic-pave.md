# Pave Diamond Ring: Automatic Pave

Learn how to craft sophisticated pavé diamond rings with precision and efficiency in this in-depth tutorial. Starting with the _Classic Shank_ command as your foundation, you’ll explore advanced design workflows to refine structure and detail. The highlight of this course is RhinoArtisan’s powerful _Automatic Pavé_ command, allowing you to position hundreds of diamonds and prongs with ease and perfect symmetry.

This tutorial focuses on balancing design aesthetics with technical accuracy—ideal for CAD jewelry designers aiming to create high-impact engagement or fashion rings with professional finishes.
