# Twist Bezel Ring

In this video, we take you step by step through the creation of a refined Open Crossover Ring using RhinoArtisan. You'll learn how to design intersecting ring arms with perfect symmetry, integrate a bezel-set center stone, and enhance the design with pavé diamonds for an elegant, modern finish.

Whether you're a jewelry designer, CAD modeler, or digital artist, this tutorial will help you master the balance between structural flow and gemstone precision.

📌 What You’ll Learn:

✔ How to model clean, symmetric crossover ring arms

✔ How to integrate a bezel setting into an open ring design

✔ How to apply diamond details with precision
