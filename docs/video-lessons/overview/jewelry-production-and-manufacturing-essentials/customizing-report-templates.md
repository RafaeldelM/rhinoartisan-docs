# Customizing Report Templates

**Welcome to RhinoArtisan!** In this tutorial, you’ll learn how to design your own **custom templates** for generating detailed and professional jewelry reports. Perfect for designers and CAD artists who want to personalize client documentation, this step-by-step guide shows how to take full control of your report layout.

📌 **What You’ll Learn:**\
✔ Generating **Breakdown Details** to extract model data\
✔ Accessing and editing the **default report template**\
✔ Creating and aligning custom data fields with **parameter-linked IDs**\
✔ Setting up **custom viewports** for Top, Front, and Perspective views\
✔ Using tools like **Array, Gumball, Scale 1D, and Align** for precise layout\
✔ Saving and organizing your templates for **future use**\
✔ Generating reports with your personalized branding and structure

📌 **Tools Used in This Video:** RhinoArtisan\
📌 **Techniques:** Report Template Design, Custom Viewports, Layout Alignment, Field Mapping, Template Saving
