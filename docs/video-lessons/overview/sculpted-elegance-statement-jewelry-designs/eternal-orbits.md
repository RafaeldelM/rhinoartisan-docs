# Eternal Orbits

Welcome to RhinoArtisan!

In this advanced tutorial, we’ll guide you through the complete process of modeling a pavé diamond ring with rails and organic SubD detailing. Starting from a simple sphere, we’ll build the ring base, create UV surfaces, flow curves along geometry, and design an intricate rail system. Step by step, you’ll learn how to shape the shank, apply QuadRemesh with symmetry, trim surfaces, offset solids, and prepare a precise structure for gem setting.

We’ll then move on to gem placement — combining Gems on Curve with Pavé to fill the design with diamonds — customizing prongs, cutters, and fine adjustments to achieve a professional, production-ready result. Finally, we’ll optimize the model by hollowing the shank and body for weight reduction, leaving you with a clean, fully prepared ring ready for rendering or manufacturing.
