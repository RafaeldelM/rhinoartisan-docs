# Bamboo Cuff Bracelet

Welcome to RhinoArtisan! In this tutorial, we’ll guide you step by step through the complete process of creating a stylish SubD bangle with pavé-set diamonds in RhinoArtisan. From building precise curves and segment divisions to shaping the organic structure with SubD tools, bridging connections, and adapting the model with Smart Flow, you’ll master a professional jewelry workflow. We’ll cover how to create a clean bangle base, add gem details with Automatic Pavé, customize prongs and cutters, and prepare a fully production-ready model. By the end, you’ll have a stunning, modern bangle ready for rendering or manufacturing.
