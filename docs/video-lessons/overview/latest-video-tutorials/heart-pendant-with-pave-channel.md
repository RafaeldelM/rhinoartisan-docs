# Heart Pendant with Pavé Channel

Welcome to RhinoArtisan! In this tutorial, we’ll guide you step by step through the creation of a **heart-shaped pendant with a pavé-set channel**, starting from a predefined curve asset and finishing with a clean, production-ready solid. You’ll learn how to refine and smooth a heart outline, convert it into SubD geometry, sculpt a functional channel for gemstones, and complete the design with precise pavé placement and cutters.

This workflow focuses on clean topology, symmetry control, and practical stone-setting techniques, making it ideal for pendants, charms, and romantic jewelry designs intended for manufacturing or high-quality rendering.
