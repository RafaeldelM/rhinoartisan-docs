# Cutters

Before applying our materials, we will use **cutters** to remove the metal that intersects with the gems. This way, we will ensure that light interacts with the material properly, allowing us to see the sparkle of our gems.

### Cutter Command

In the _**Diamonds**_ tab, click on the _**Cutter**_ command.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Cutter.png)

### Layers Panel

Now use the _**Layers**_ panel to select the gems. To do this, **right-click** on the **colors** designated for gems specifically, the first two orange ones.

Once the gems are selected, click on the _**Selector**_.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Cutter.png)

### Parameters

Once the gems are selected and added to the _**Selector**_(1), select the **third preset** (2). Then, change the _**Height Drill (%)**_**&#x20;to zero** (3), so it doesn’t cut through the metal we don’t need. We just want it to cover the gem.\
<br>

<figure><img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Cutter_parameters.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Metals

Now, we will **select the metals** by right-clicking on the _Metal_ layer (blue color).

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Select-metals.png)

### Boolean Command

To cut the metal using our cutters, go to the _**Solid**_&#x74;ab (1) and select the _**Boolean Difference**_ command (2).

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson-1-Boolean.jpg)

Then **select the cutters** by clicking on them, and finally **press&#x20;**_**Enter**_ again to apply the boolean operation.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Boolean-Result.png)
