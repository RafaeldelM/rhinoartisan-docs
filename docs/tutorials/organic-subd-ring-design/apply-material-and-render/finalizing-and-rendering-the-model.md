# Finalizing and Rendering the Model

### **Adjust Render Settings**

* Open the **Rendering and Animation Panel**.
* Set the desired **Resolution** and **Quality** parameters.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/image-13.png)

### **Start the Render**

Click **Render** in the **Rendering and Animation Panel** to generate the final image.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Render-1.png)

You’ve successfully completed the tutorial on creating and posing multiple rings for rendering. The final model is now ready to be showcased on social media or in a portfolio. Take your time to experiment with materials, lighting, and poses to further enhance your renders.
