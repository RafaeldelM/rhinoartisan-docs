# Halo Setting

### **Step 1:  Halo**

Select the gem, then go to the toolbar and click on the "Halo" command.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-2-1-1024x138.png)

### **Step 2: Library**

Choose the HA012 model for the halo setting.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/image-5-1.png)

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/image-5-1-1-1024x556.png)

### **Step 3:  Halo Parameters**

Press "Edit" and adjust the metal profile parameters:

* Width: 2.10 millimeters.
* Distance to gem: 0.20 millimeters.
* Z position: -0.30 millimeters.
* Rotation: 10 millimeters.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-3-461x1024.png)
