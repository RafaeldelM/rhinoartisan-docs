# Use Elements from curator

Now, let's start creating our new earrings.

### **Step 1: Reuse Elements**

For this, select our saved basket in our new category “RhinoArtisan Tutorial” in the Curator Panel, as you know, the panel at the bottom left corner of the screen.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-12h44m08s471.png)

### **Step 2: Shaded**

For a better visualization of the model, we will use the shaded mode, which you can find in the third button at the lower right corner of each viewport. Switch to this mode.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-12h41m29s579.png)

### **Step 3: Zoom Extents**

Additionally, to center the element, go to Perspective, select Pan, Zoom, and Rotate, and then Zoom Extents All.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-17-12h45m51s265.png)

### **Step 4: Add the subD**

Now, add our subD from the Curator panel.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/vlcsnap-2024-07-16-14h45m11s587.jpg)
