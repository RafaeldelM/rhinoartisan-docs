# Apply Materials: Ring

### **Step 1**: Apply Gems Materials

On the ring model, select two gems after each green gem, leaving one gem unselected between each pair.&#x20;

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/GemsRing-1024x356.png)

> _Using **Shift** you can select multiple elements._

### **Step 2: Ground Material**

Apply **Tourmaline Rose** to these selected gems and apply **Citrine** to the remaining ones.

**Step 3:** Apply the **Basic Reflection** **Black** material to the floor.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ground-1024x271.png)

### **Step 4: Advanced Adjustment**

In the **Materials** panel, adjust the reflection polish to 88%.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/MaterialPanel-203x300.png)

> [Material panel documentation](https://docs.mcneel.com/rhino/8/help/en-us/commands/materials.htm?Highlight=material)

### **Step 5: Render Parameters**

In the **Render and Animation** panel, set the resolution to 4K and the quality to 52.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Render_animation-1024x452.png)

### **Step 6: Environments Panel**

Right-click on **Studio** in the **Environments** panel and choose **Set as Custom Reflective Environment**.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Environments-1-1024x452.png)

> [Environment panel documentation](https://docs.mcneel.com/rhino/8/help/en-us/commands/environments.htm?Highlight=environment)

### **Step 7: Render**

In the **Rendering and Animation** panel click on Render.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Render-Final-1024x576.png)
