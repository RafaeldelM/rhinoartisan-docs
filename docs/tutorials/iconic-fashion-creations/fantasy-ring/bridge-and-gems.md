# Bridge and Gems

### **Step 1: Bridge Parameters**

For the bridge, change the thickness to 0.90 millimeters and the diameter to 2.40 millimeters.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-18-at-12.49.02.png)

### **Step 2 : Gems**

Now add gems to our shank in the gems tab of this panel. Change the gem size to 1.20, angle to 80 degrees, Inner Height to 0.5 millimeters, Bright cut to 0.01 millimeters.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-18-at-12.55.22.png)
