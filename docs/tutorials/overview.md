# Overview

We are pleased to present a carefully curated selection of tutorials to guide you step-by-step, from foundational concepts to advanced techniques. Each topic is available in both text with images and video formats, allowing you to choose the option that best suits your learning preferences. Should you have any questions at any time, please do not hesitate to contact our support team; we would be delighted to assist you.

### From RhinoArtisan to Keyshot <a href="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/course/keyshot-starter" class="button primary">Start Learning</a>



Unlock the full potential of your jewelry designs with our From RhinoArtisan to Keyshot course. This course guides you step-by-step through preparing your RhinoArtisan models for breathtaking renders using KeyShot, ensuring every detail shines with perfection.&#x20;

Start by organizing and importing essential resources, from 3DM models to materials and environments. Learn efficient file management techniques to streamline your workflow, making it easy to access and apply the resources you need. Master the integration of RhinoArtisan with KeyShot through Live Linking, ensuring your models update dynamically without interrupting your creative flow.&#x20;

Dive into KeyShot’s intuitive tools to position elements, refine materials, and enhance lighting. You’ll explore advanced techniques like using multi-materials for metals, optimizing diamond realism with increased ray bounces, and creating visually stunning environments to elevate your designs. Discover how to quickly edit and adjust your models in RhinoArtisan and see the changes instantly reflected in KeyShot, empowering you to experiment and perfect every design detail. By the end of this course, you’ll confidently render professional-quality images that capture the beauty and craftsmanship of your creations—ideal for portfolios, social media, and client presentations. Experience the seamless synergy of RhinoArtisan and KeyShot to bring your jewelry visions to life.
