# Lighting and Environments

**Objective:** Optimize the scene’s lighting and environment for realistic reflections and highlights.

**Adjust Lighting**

* Go to the **Lighting tab** in the **Project panel**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (51).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

&#x20;

* Set **Ray Bounces** to 17 for enhanced diamond brilliance.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (52).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Change Environment**

* Download the custom environment from the course resources and place it in the `Documents > KeyShot > Environments` folder.
* In KeyShot, refresh the environment list.
* Apply the new environment to the scene, double click.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (53).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* In the **Environment** tab, change the Background to **Color**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (54).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
