# Customizing the Model

**Objective:** Edit one earring for variety in the final render.

**Modify the Basket in RhinoArtisan**

Now, without closing Keyshot, go back to RhinoArtisan. Select the **basket** (1) of one earring and **click on edit** (2). Change the **prong count** from four to **six** (3).

**Update KeyShot**

Click the **Update Scene button** in RhinoArtisan to sync changes with KeyShot.

Sometimes, the material may not be applied after a change, but it has an easy solution. Copying and pasting a material is very simple, as there is a keyboard shortcut for this function: hold down the **Shift key + left mouse button** on the material you want to copy, and **Shift + right mouse button** on the part where you want to paste the material, and that’s it.
