# The Project Panel

The **Project Panel** is a central feature in KeyShot Studio, offering powerful tools to manage and adjust all elements of your scene. It allows designers to fine-tune their jewelry models, apply realistic materials, control lighting, set camera views, and perform post-production edits—all in one place. Mastering this panel is essential for creating stunning, photorealistic renderings of jewelry pieces designed in RhinoArtisan.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Scene**: Displays the hierarchical structure of models and components. Select, organize, and modify jewelry pieces here.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Materials**: Edit applied materials, including color, shine, transparency, and texture for realistic rendering.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Environment**: Adjust global lighting using HDRI maps to influence reflections and shadows.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Camera**: Manage camera settings, including focal length, depth of field, and angles to capture every detail.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (27).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Image**: Post-process rendered images by tweaking exposure, contrast, and color corrections for professional results.
