# Turn upside down

## ​<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/G6_07_DIAMOND_GEM_UPSIDEDOW (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Turn upside down <a href="#flip" id="flip"></a>

This command allows us to change the orientation plane of the gems. By default, RhinoArtisan automatically orients the gems, but in some cases, even if they are geometrically oriented correctly, they may be internally reversed.
