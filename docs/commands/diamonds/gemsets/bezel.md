# Bezel

## <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/G4_02_DIAMOND_BEZEL.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Bezel

The **Bezel** command in our software is designed to give you complete control over the creation of a bezel setting, ensuring a perfect fit for your gemstones. It is organized into three distinct tabs, each offering essential tools and parameters for precise customization.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (591).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**1. Parameters Tab**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Group 7270 (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In the **Parameters** tab, you can define the key characteristics of your bezel:

* **Section Curve**: View and select the curve used to shape the bezel. You have the option to choose a different curve from your assets collection or edit the current one to refine its shape.
* **Width and Height**: Set the width and height of the bezel to match the dimensions required for your design.
* **Over Girdle Height**: This parameter adds extra metal above the girdle, which is crucial for accommodating any adjustments needed during setting. This extra height allows you to hammer the metal securely around the stone and account for metal removal during filing and polishing.
* **Girdle**: Define the girdle’s thickness, which is essential for ensuring that the bezel is robust enough to support the gemstone while considering the material that will be removed during finishing.
* **Gem Inside**: Allow a small margin in the girdle to ensure the gemstone fits smoothly into the bezel. This margin not only helps the gem to seat properly but also saves time during the setting process. By providing this slight clearance, you minimize the need for excessive metal removal or heavy hammering, leading to a cleaner and more efficient setting.

**2. Support Tab**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Bezel Post.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Support** tab is used to add and customize support features:

* **Earring Post Support**: If your bezel design requires an earring post, this tab allows you to integrate a post support. Adjust the width and height of the supports so that the bezel is pre-configured with the correct location for the post, ensuring a seamless design.

**3. Cutter and Airgaps Tab**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Bezel 3 Parameters.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

:::info
Goldsmith Tip : When designing a bezel, precise measurements of the gemstone are crucial for ensuring a proper fit. It's essential to account for the exact dimensions of the stone to create a bezel that accommodates it comfortably.
:::

The **Cutter and Airgaps** tab lets you add aesthetic and functional details to the bezel:

* **Cutter**: Create precise cuts in the bezel’s girdle to enhance its design or to facilitate adjustments. This can be useful for adding decorative elements or improving the fit of the stone.
* **Airgaps**: Introduce airgaps in the bezel’s middle section. These gaps can be used for both aesthetic purposes and to ensure that the bezel is lightweight and comfortable.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (592).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Keep in mind that you can save the element you create in the gallery for further designs.

:::tip
This element is editable, meaning it can be modified anytime.
:::
