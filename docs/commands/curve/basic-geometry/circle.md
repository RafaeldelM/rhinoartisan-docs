# Circle

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/G1_05_DRAW_CIRCLE.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Circle

The Circle command draws a circle from a center location and a radius.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_01_DRAW_DIAMETER.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Diameter

Draws the circle from the two ends of its diameter.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_02_DRAW_POINTS.png" alt="" data-size="line"> By 3 Points

Draws a circle through three points on the circumference.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_03_DRAW_AROUND_CURVE.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Around Curve

Draws a circle perpendicular to a curve.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_04_DRAW_TANGENT_RADIUS.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Tangent, Tangent, Radius

Draws a circle tangent to two curves and a given radius.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_05_DRAW_TANGENT.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Tangent 3 Curves

Draws a circle tangent to three curves.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_06_DRAW_VERTICAL_CPLANE.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Vertical CPlane: Center

Draws a circle perpendicular to the construction plane at a given center.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_07_DRAW_VERTICAL_CPLANE_DIAMETER.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Vertical CPlane: Diameter

Draws a circle perpendicular to the construction plane with a given diameter.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_08_DRAW_DEFORMABLE.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Deformable

Draws a NURBS approximation of the curve will be created with the degree and number of points you specify.

### <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/CIRCLE_09_DRAW_FIT_POINTS.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Fit Points

Draws a circle by fitting to selected point objects, curve and surface [control points](https://docs.mcneel.com/rhino/8/help/en-us/popup_moreinformation/controlpoint.htm), and mesh vertices.
