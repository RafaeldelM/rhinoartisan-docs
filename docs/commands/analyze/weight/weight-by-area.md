# Weight by Area

## <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/WEIGHT_AREA (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Weight by Area

This tool is ingeniously designed to calculate the weight of plating based on an object's surface area. It proves immensely useful for designers and manufacturers seeking accuracy in metal coating processes, allowing for a more precise estimation of the metal weight used in electroplating.
