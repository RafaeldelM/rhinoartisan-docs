# Eternity Ring

## <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/RING_ETERNOTY.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Eternity Ring

The Eternity command enables jewelers to create a wide variety of eternity bands, including Full, Half, Three-Quarter, Three-Stone, Five-Stone, and Nine-Stone designs, all within a single command. For bands that are not Full Eternities, you can customize the remaining shank by adding your preferred sections and adjusting the parameters to achieve a harmonious design.



<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/eternity.PNG" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Parameters

The **Eternity** command's parameter panel is divided into **four different tabs** to help you customize every aspect of the eternity band design.

The first tab focuses on **gem distribution**, allowing you to choose between options such as **Full**, **Half**, **Three-Quarters**, **Five Stones**, **Seven Stones**, or **Nine Stones**. This flexibility lets you determine how much of the band is covered by gemstones. You can also select the **gemstone cut**, with options like **Round**, **Princess**, **Radiant**, **Oval**, and **Emerald**.

In addition, this tab provides settings to define the **gem sizes** and the **distance between the stones**, as well as the option to **adjust the gems' position along the Z-axis** for precise placement and alignment.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (175).png" alt="" width="263"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The second tab in the **Eternity** command's parameter panel focuses on the **prong settings**. Here, you can customize various aspects of the prongs used to secure the gemstones. The prongs can be created as either **straight or curved**, and you have full control over the **distance between them** at both the **top and bottom**, allowing you to adjust the **inclination**.

You can also set the **height of the prongs above the girdle** of the gems, ensuring the gemstones are securely held in place. Additionally, the tab provides parameters for specifying the **diameter at different sections** of the prong, enabling precise control over the prong's shape and size

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (176).png" alt="" width="260"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The third tab in the **Eternity** command's parameter panel is dedicated to the **rails** of the eternity ring. You can choose to create the ring with either **one or two rails**, and customize their shape as **round**, **square**, or **oval**. This tab allows you to adjust all relevant parameters to fine-tune the rails to your desired dimensions. In the **Rails – Profile** option, you can now select between a **round** or a **rectangular** shape, allowing you to adapt the rails to the desired style of the ringensuring that the overall design of the ring perfectly matches your specifications and aesthetic preferences.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (763).png" alt="" width="234"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The last tab in the **Eternity** command's parameter panel pertains to the **shank** of the eternity ring, specifically applicable when the **Full** option is not selected in the first tab. In this tab, you can customize the parameters for the sections of the shank, allowing you to shape and design it according to your preferences. This flexibility enables you to create a unique look that complements the overall style of the eternity band.
