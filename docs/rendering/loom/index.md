# Loom

#### Loom: Intelligent Photorealistic Rendering for RhinoArtisan

**Loom** is our advanced AI designed to transform your RhinoArtisan designs into photorealistic images quickly and effortlessly. With Loom, your ideas come to life in seconds, capturing every detail of your creations with precision and style.

Within RA, Loom is fully integrated through its **dedicated panel**, allowing you to:

* **Capture your design** and send it to Loom to generate the image.
* **Communicate with the AI** using natural descriptions: specify the type of metal, gemstones, enamel, or any other material you imagine. Loom interprets your instructions and turns them into high-quality images.
* **Control the composition**: adjust the position of your design, request different views, and define the environment or scene for your image.
* **Explore endless possibilities**: from subtle changes in lighting and materials to complete scenes that showcase your design, all through an intuitive workflow without any technical complexity.

Loom doesn’t just render—**it brings your designs to life**, letting you visualize and present your pieces professionally and realistically, without needing advanced rendering skills.
