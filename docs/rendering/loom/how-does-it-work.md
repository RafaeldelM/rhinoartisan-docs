# How does it work ?

#### Loom User Guide

**Step 1: Prepare Your Design**

Before sending your design to Loom, it’s important to prepare it properly to get the best results. This step is simple but ensures high-quality, photorealistic images.

1. **Set the metal of your design**:
   * For the clearest contrast when capturing a screenshot, we recommend selecting **silver or white gold** in the **Outliner panel**.
   * If your design includes **different metal tones**, select all the relevant metals in the Outliner to reflect the variations in the final image.
2. **Gemstones and materials**:
   * By default, gemstones in RhinoArtisan appear as **diamonds** in the **Retail Visualization Mode** (accessible in the Outliner).
   * You don’t need to assign the final materials here—Loom will generate them based on the **description you provide in your prompt**, allowing you to define gem types, colors, or other materials freely.

**Tip:** Preparing your design in this way ensures that the screenshot captured for Loom has clear shapes and contrasts, giving the AI a strong base to generate a realistic image.

**Step 2: Open the Loom Panel and Capture a Snapshot**

1. **Open the Loom panel**:
   * <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/FILE_PANEL_LOOM_DARK.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Go to the **File menu** and select **Loom**.
   * We recommend **anchoring this panel in the right sidebar** alongside your other RhinoArtisan panels for easy access. You will see it with this icon : <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/TAB_LOOM_DARK (2).png" alt="" data-size="line">
2. **Capture your design**:
   * &#x20;<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/LOOM_NEW_DARK.png" alt="" data-size="line"> At the bottom of the Loom panel, you’ll see several icons. The **first icon from the left** is **“Capture Snapshot”**.
   * Click on this icon, and RhinoArtisan will generate a **snapshot of your design**.
   * The captured image will appear as the **selected image inside the panel**.

This snapshot is the **base image that Loom will use** to create your photorealistic render.

**Step 3: Write Your Prompt and Customize the Scene**

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/copilot_send_white.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Now it’s time to **write your prompt** for Loom. This is where you define how you want your design to appear in the final image.

1. **Define the materials**:
   * Specify the metals, gemstones, enamels, ceramics, or any other materials that compose your design. Loom will interpret your description to generate realistic textures and colors.
2. **Define the background or scene**:
   * For a simple image, you can choose a **plain background** like white, grey, or any other solid color.
   * For a more complex composition, you can describe a **scene**: for example, a marble table, a wooden surface, or decorative elements such as flowers. Here, **your imagination is the limit**—Loom can create detailed environments that enhance your design.

**Tip:** The more precise and clear your prompt is, the better Loom can capture the materials, lighting, and mood you envision for your design.

**Step 4: Send the Prompt to Loom and Refine the Image**

Once your prompt is ready, **send it to Loom**. The AI will interpret your instructions and quickly generate the desired image.

You can also **add additional prompts to modify the image**, for example:

* Request **different views of the design** in a single image if you need a more technical render.
* Change the **perspective or angle** of the design.
* Modify the **background or scene**.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/copilot_receipts_white.png" alt="" data-size="line"> In addition, you can **save the prompts and results you find most satisfying into the “Recipe Book.”** This allows you to reuse proven prompts with other designs, ensuring consistent results and saving time in future projects.

Again, **your imagination and needs are the limit**. Loom allows you to refine and experiment with your renders until they perfectly match your vision.
