# Can I share designs with customers who don’t have an account?

Yes, you can share designs with customers who don’t have a RhinoArtisan Connect account. Just generate a share link, and your clients will be able to view, interact with, and even customize the designs directly from that link—no registration or installation required.

You’ll also receive notifications when someone views your design, and you can see how many people have accessed it. This feature is perfect for sales follow-up and tracking customer interest in real time.

If you wish, you can also optionally protect access with a password for extra security.
