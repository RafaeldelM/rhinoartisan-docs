# What platforms is Connect available on? (Windows, Mac, mobile, web, etc.)

RhinoArtisan Connect is fully available on the web, allowing access from any device—Windows, macOS, or Linux—without installation. We also offer **native desktop apps for Windows and macOS**, along with mobile apps for **Android** and **iOS**, so you can view, share, and present designs smoothly from your desktop, phone, or tablet—even in stores or at events.
