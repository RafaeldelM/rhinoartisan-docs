# Do you offer onboarding or training?

Yes, we offer both onboarding sessions and personalized training for new users and teams. Our goal is to help you get started with RhinoArtisan Connect quickly and efficiently.

The onboarding session is completely free and includes step-by-step guidance to set up the platform, import your first designs, and answer any initial questions. If your company or school needs it, we can also arrange customized training sessions for your entire team, either online or in person.
