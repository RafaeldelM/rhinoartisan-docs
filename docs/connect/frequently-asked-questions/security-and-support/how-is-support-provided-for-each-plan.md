# How is support provided for each plan?

Support in RhinoArtisan Connect varies depending on your plan:

* Free plan: Email support, with a response time of up to 72 business hours.
* Pro plan: Priority support by email and phone, with faster response times.
* Enterprise plan: Dedicated and personalized support, including top-priority attention by email and phone, direct contact with the technical team, and the option to arrange online or video call support sessions if needed.<br>

All users also have access to official documentation, tutorials, and self-service resources.
