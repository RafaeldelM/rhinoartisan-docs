# Is training or documentation available for new users?

Yes, we provide comprehensive documentation and training resources for new users of RhinoArtisan Connect. You can access manuals and guides at[ https://docs.rhinoartisan.com](https://docs.rhinoartisan.com), and find courses, video tutorials, and further training at[ www.rhinoartisan.com/learn](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/learn).

We also offer regular online training sessions and webinars to help teams get the most out of the platform. If your company or school needs customized training, we can arrange tailored sessions as well.
