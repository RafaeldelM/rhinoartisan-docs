# Who is RhinoArtisan Connect for?

RhinoArtisan Connect is designed for jewelry stores, manufacturers, designers, and retailers who want to offer a more interactive and personalized jewelry design experience, both in-store and online. It’s ideal for teams looking to collaborate in real time, share 3D collections with their customers, and leverage the power of artificial intelligence to accelerate the design and sales process.

It’s also a great tool for independent studios, schools, and any professional seeking to innovate in the presentation and customization of jewelry designs.
