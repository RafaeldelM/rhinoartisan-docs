# Do you offer special pricing for educational institutions?

Yes, we offer free access to RhinoArtisan Connect for schools and universities that have an active RhinoArtisan Lab School license. This allows both teachers and students to enjoy all the educational features of Connect, including collaboration tools and collection management.

AI features are available to these accounts under fair use conditions, tailored to educational settings.

If your institution already has a RhinoArtisan Lab School license, contact us to activate Connect access. If not, we can advise you on how to obtain an educational license.
