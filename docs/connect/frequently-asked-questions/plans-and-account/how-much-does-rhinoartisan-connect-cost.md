# How much does RhinoArtisan Connect cost?

RhinoArtisan Connect offers three plans to fit any organization’s needs:

* Free: No cost, ideal for individuals and small trials (1 user, essential features).
* Pro: €99 per month per organization, with all professional features, up to 25 users, and AI access.\
  Also available as an annual plan for €999 per year (save two months).
* Enterprise: From €399 per month per organization, for large companies and chains, with unlimited users, advanced integrations, and dedicated support. Exact pricing depends on your requirements.<br>

All prices are per organization, not per user, so your entire team can benefit from the platform with a single subscription.
