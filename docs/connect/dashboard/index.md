# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the central control panel of the platform—designed to give administrators and authorized users complete visibility and control over their organization’s data, users, integrations, and AI-powered tools.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Home.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

More than just a management interface, the Dashboard acts as the **operational hub** for all key functions related to jewelry design, production, and business management. Whether you’re updating company information, onboarding new team members, integrating with external services like **Nivoda**, or leveraging intelligent virtual assistants trained in CAD and gemology, the Dashboard is where it all begins.

### Key Capabilities

**Organization Management**: View and edit company information used in documents, reports, and automated communications.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Organizacion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**User and Role Administration:** Invite team members, assign roles (Owner, Supervisor), and manage access levels with ease.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Accounts (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**External Integrations:** Seamlessly connect to third-party services like **Nivoda** to access global diamond inventories and streamline sourcing.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Integrations.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**AI Assistant Interaction:** Use domain-specialized AI agents to get expert help in 3D modeling, pricing, gem grading, documentation, and more.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/AI (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
